# EvolveTS Gate 1 dev results (first complete run)

## What this is
Tuned hand-designed online updaters (Equal, Follow-the-Leader, Hedge, Fixed-Share, AdaHedge) replayed over cached forecasts of the expert pool, on three dev datasets (ETTh1, ECL, synthetic-dev), with and without three foundation models (Chronos-2, TiRex, TimesFM 2.5) in the pool. MASE, lower is better. All numbers below come from the ledger of one run and the report generated from it.

## Headline reading
1. **Classical pool only:** tuned Fixed-Share is best everywhere and clearly beats the best single expert (ETTh1 0.925 vs 1.038; ECL 0.920 vs 1.019; synthetic 0.848 vs 0.896). Mixing experts pays off when the experts are weak.
2. **With foundation models in the pool, the updater adds almost nothing over the best single foundation expert:** ETTh1 0.8133 (Hedge) vs 0.8154 (Chronos-2 alone); ECL 0.6837 vs 0.6889 (TiRex alone); synthetic 0.7771 vs 0.7766 (Chronos-2 alone). The best tuned hand-designed updater is essentially "pick the best foundation model".
3. **Headroom that remains** (mean gap from the best tuned baseline, with 95% bootstrap intervals in the tables): against a switching oracle with at most 8 switches, ETTh1 0.022, ECL 0.011, synthetic 0.030; with at most 32 switches, 0.059, 0.027, 0.054. The per-origin best expert (select_mase) looks at the answer and is not reachable: gaps 0.157, 0.080, 0.079.
4. **Synthetic worlds:** the true-best forecast has MASE 0.7223 and the best tuned baseline with foundation models is 0.7771 (gap 0.055); with the classical pool alone the gap is 0.126. So the planted mismatches leave measurable room.

## Caveats (faithful reading)
- Dev numbers: the configurations were chosen on the same series, so the baselines' fit is flattered.
- ETTh1 and ECL results with foundation models are flagged leakage-risk (a foundation model may have seen these datasets in pretraining).
- Several selected configurations sit at the edge of the tuning grid (Hedge eta 0.015625 on ETTh1, simple and pooled; 0.03125 on ECL pooled), so those baselines may be slightly under-tuned.
- The oracles bound single-expert selection only; they are not a floor for weighted combinations (Fixed-Share beats "best expert per series" on the classical pool).
